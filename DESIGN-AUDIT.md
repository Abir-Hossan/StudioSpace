## Functional details restored with the layout

- Space Grotesk and Inter weights 400, 500, 600 and 700 are bundled locally, with their Open Font Licenses. Headings no longer depend on a system fallback or a regular-weight font pretending to be semibold.
- Studios can store usable floor area, grid height, power supply, acoustics, access, parking and stage rules. Existing records with no specifications show “Not provided”; no technical claims are fabricated.
- Precision filters query MongoDB for amenities, capacity, rating, ceiling height, location, price and date availability. Selected filters remain in the URL.
- Listing pause/resume is persisted. Paused studios disappear from public results and reject new booking requests, while existing history is retained.
- Listing CSV, production-calendar ICS, studio specification catalog PDF and aggregate booking-summary PDF exports produce real files. Booking PDFs are clearly marked as summaries, not payment receipts.
- Profile title, bio, contact/portfolio information and planning preferences persist in MongoDB. Email remains protected from unverified changes.
- Create-studio drafts save on the current device, under the current account’s identifier. The UI reports a draft only after storage succeeds; it does not claim automatic cloud saving.
- Gallery navigation, share-link copying, contact information, filter sheets, status tabs and profile tabs are connected to real behavior.

## Intentional adaptations from the prototype

The references include state-preview switches, mock counts, instant-booking labels, escrow/payment statements, insurance limits, union certifications, staffing claims and fictional testimonials. Those are not live services in this project. Their visual positions are retained where useful, with real data, an accurate explanation, or a working alternative. Prototype-only Default/Error/Success, Loading and Empty-state switches were not exposed as product controls.

Community imagery illustrates creator roles; it does not identify real employees. The location graphic is labelled illustrative and links to actual listing locations. No exact coordinates are invented for unrecognized cities. Source photos remain approximately 512 pixels wide, so matching the source images does not remove their resolution limit.

Existing user records were preserved. The fresh seed includes a Frame 24 reference listing and its exact five-photo gallery/specifications for new local databases. The seed does not replace an existing database.

- The public/private route suite covers **126 route-and-viewport combinations** at 320, 375, 430, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 pixels, without horizontal document overflow.
- Representative desktop/mobile screenshots were captured in the separately delivered `design-review` directory. Those capture runs reported **no unhandled page errors**.
